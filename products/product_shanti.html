SHANTI RITUAL

“A ritual to slow down and soften into the present.”

2,550

Shanti brings together wild botanicals, plant waters, forest honey, and thoughtfully crafted tools to create a moment of pause and reconnection.

Every ingredient is agrochemical-free and undergoes energy work, infused with prāna and healing frequencies to amplify the essence of Shanti. Rooted in nature and crafted with intention, every element comes together to bring a little more stillness into your everyday.
1

WHAT'S INSIDE

Rūḥ | The Herbal Blend

The heart of the ritual. A floral blend of chamomile from Uttarakhand, rose from Maharashtra, fennel from Gujarat, and cardamom from South India.

Arkā | The Plant Water Mist

A blend of rose and chamomile herbal waters with glycerites, infused with prāna and healing frequencies to complement your Shanti ritual.

Madhu | Forest Honey

Sourced from the Karanj forests of Jharkhand, Madhu brings the wild sweetness of the forest to your cup. Its distinctive flavour and subtly cooling character complement the floral notes of Rūḥ.

The Ritual Tools

A food-grade spoon and a spacious, pyramid-shaped food-grade strainer that allows the herbs room to unfurl as they steep.

The Bamboo Keepsake Box

Handwoven by artisans in Dumka village, Jharkhand, this reusable bamboo box celebrates traditional craftsmanship and conscious living. Keep it, reuse it, and give it a new purpose beyond the ritual.

YOUR SHANTI RITUAL

01

Let the botanicals unfurl

Place Rūḥ in the strainer and steep in hot water for 5 minutes.

02

Infuse and sweeten

Add approximately 10 pumps of Arkā and half a teaspoon of Madhu. Stir gently.

03

Savour the moment

Breathe in the florals. Savour each sip. Let the world grow quieter.

Made for a second cup

Rūḥ can be brewed twice, letting you return to the ritual once more.

Rooted in Nature. Crafted with Intention.

From agrochemical-free botanicals and forest-sourced ingredients to prāna energy work and handwoven craftsmanship, every element of Shanti is thoughtfully brought together.

More than a cup of tea, Shanti is an invitation to slow down, reconnect with the present, and make space for stillness.
